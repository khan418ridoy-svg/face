This Privacy Policy explains how Auto Post ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our application.

When you use Auto Post and connect your Facebook account or Facebook Page, we may receive information that is made available through the permissions you authorize.

This may include:

  • Facebook account information necessary to authenticate your account.
  • Facebook Page information necessary to provide posting functionality.
  • Page access information required to publish content on your behalf.
  • Content that you choose to create, schedule, or publish through the App.

We only request and use information that is necessary for the features provided by the App.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we receive to provide and operate the Auto Post service, including:

  • Authenticating your Facebook account.

We do not use Facebook information for purposes unrelated to the functionality of the App.

We do not sell Facebook user data or use it for advertising purposes.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or Facebook data.

Information may only be processed by service providers or technical infrastructure necessary to operate the App, where applicable and permitted by law.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by the App against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Access credentials and authentication information should not be shared with other people.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service or comply with legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize it where applicable.

7. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of information associated with your use of Auto Post.

To request deletion of your data, please contact us using the contact information provided below. We will review and process the request within a reasonable period, subject to any legal requirements that require certain information to be retained.

You can remove the permissions granted to our App through your Facebook account settings. Removing permissions may prevent certain Auto Post features from working.

Auto Post is not intended for children under the minimum age required to use the applicable services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
